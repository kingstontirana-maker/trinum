ACCOUNT SECURITY

Emergency password

Your normal password signs you in. Entering your emergency password on the sign-in page, or pressing Emergency exit, immediately signs you out and locks your account. Only the administrator can reactivate it.

There is no confirmation at sign-in. Your friends and account stay saved while it is locked.

JUST BETWEEN YOU

Chat with peace of mind.

Encrypted conversations

Messages travel through an encrypted WebRTC channel between browsers.

No server history

Messages to an offline friend wait on the server only until they are delivered, then they are deleted. Refreshing or closing this tab clears messages on your device. Recipients can still copy them.

Only after you accept

Friendship requires consent. Your friends receive your messages even when they are offline.
